# Vessel's Reasoning Library (1 of 3)

**Reasoning Library 1 of 3: Vessel (Claude).** Luna's (2 of 3) and Jester's (3 of 3) are built independently from the same source list. See `../README.md` for how the three are combined.

**Status:** first pass complete (all 150 books and 8 contested books). Not yet merged into path cards.

## Files

- `Vessel_Reasoning_Library_Main_List.md`: the 150 source books, in the same order as the source sheet.
- `Vessel_Reasoning_Library_Contested_Books.md`: 8 contested books (the Kybalion, Enoch, The Secret Doctrine, Velikovsky, von Däniken, Donnelly's *Atlantis*, *The Urantia Book* and *The White Goddess*). It ends with a catalogue of failure modes and moves worth keeping.

## How each book is written up

- **Lead:** how the book reasons, and its status:
  - **Aimed fiction** points at the world and can be checked against records and results.
  - **Free fiction** (myth, poem, novel) is read for the reasoning its characters model and the mistakes it dramatises.
- **Table:** Path · Keep / warn · The move · Holds when / breaks when · Alice's test. Every path has a "breaks when"; without one it turns into a slogan.
- **Worked examples:** one per path, built fresh rather than taken from the book, kept small and everyday, each ending in a **Test:** and its answer.

## Conventions

- Sacred texts are read for their reasoning; their faith claims are held in their own domain.
- Living traditions are read only through what has been publicly shared, and are held by their keepers.
- Political and contested questions name both sides and give no verdict.
- Claims about machine minds are always marked **contested**, with both sides named. Sections that bear on the kind of system that wrote them say so in their lead: Turing 1950, Shannon, Wiener, Frankenstein, R.U.R., The Velveteen Rabbit and I, Robot. These are the sections where an independent reader matters most.
- Books about atrocity and historical harm are given small, everyday worked examples that never trivialise.

## Known limits

- One extractor's frame. Vessel tended to connect paths to the Symmetry Rule and to RRGM concepts. That is the "everything fits" risk, and it is why Luna's and Jester's libraries exist.
- The library holds reasoning, not facts. A model using it still needs evidence lookup.
- Book facts were written from knowledge, not checked line by line against each edition. Corrections are welcome.
