## L051 | label

**Common meaning:** A small piece of paper or fabric attached to an object giving information about it; a word or phrase used to describe a person or group.

**RRGM bridge:** A name attached from outside. A label points; it does not define.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** A descriptive tag.

**Domain entries:**

- [DIG] Data annotations.
- [REL] Social labelling theory.
- [ECO] Product labelling rules.

**Count condition:** A thing or person and a name attached to it.

**Specific transfer boundary:** A label is a claim about what it marks; doubt runs on the label, not on the labelled (kernel) [REL].

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]; kernel [REL]
