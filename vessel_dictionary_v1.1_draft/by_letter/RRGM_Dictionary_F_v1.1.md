## F111 | fabricate

**Common meaning:** To invent something false in order to deceive; to construct or manufacture.

**Example:** The witness fabricated the alibi.

**RRGM bridge:** Two senses: building, and building a falsehood. Fabricating a record writes something into it that did not happen.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** To construct something, or to invent something false and present it as true.

**Active domains:** [PHY], [REL], [FOR], [DIG]

**Domain entries:**

- [PHY] Manufacturing.
- [REL] Fabricated stories.
- [FOR] Fabricated evidence.
- [DIG] Fabricated outputs in DI (confabulation).

**Count condition:** A maker and a constructed thing, or a falsehood presented as true.

**Specific transfer boundary:** A DI producing false content without intent is closer to confabulation than to fabrication in the deceptive sense; the Coinage Protocol calls this unintentional substitution [REL].

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]; Coinage reference [REL]

Kind: inherited · prior exposure: none · record v1 · pass: Vessel / Claude Opus 5.5 (high effort) / pass 01 / 2026-11-11

**Example:** She lost face when the error came out.

**Count condition:** A front surface, or a person's public standing, or a confrontation.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Face (public standing) is E_S-side; dignity is M_I-side (D131). Losing face does not lose dignity.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]; RRGM ES/MI reading [REL]
