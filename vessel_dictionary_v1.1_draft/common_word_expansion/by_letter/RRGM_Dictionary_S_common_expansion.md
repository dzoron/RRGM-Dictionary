## C2-S-0001 | such

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** Of the kind already mentioned or about to be mentioned; also to so great a degree.

**Example:** I've never seen such a storm; such questions are hard to answer.

**RRGM bridge:** Such points back or forward to a kind, carrying the description across without repeating it.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** Of the type referred to, or to that degree.

**Active domains:** [LIN]

**Domain entries:**

- [LIN] A determiner and intensifier; "such as" introduces examples.

**Count condition:** A kind or degree that has been, or is about to be, specified.

**Specific transfer boundary:** "Such as" introduces examples, not a complete list.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

## C2-S-0002 | something

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** An unspecified or unknown thing; also a thing of some importance.

**Example:** There's something in my shoe; she's really something.

**RRGM bridge:** Something reserves a place for a thing that is present but not yet named.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** An indefinite reference to a thing, known to exist but not identified.

**Active domains:** [LIN], [COG]

**Domain entries:**

- [LIN] An indefinite pronoun; "something like" approximates.
- [COG] Marks a thing noticed but not yet classified.

**Count condition:** A thing whose existence is asserted while its identity is left open.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Naming something as "something" does not identify it; vague reference can hide a gap in what is actually known.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-S-0003 | sure

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** Confident that something is true; certain to happen; also used informally to mean yes.

**Example:** Are you sure the door is locked? — Sure, I checked.

**RRGM bridge:** Sure reports the speaker's confidence, which is a state of the speaker, not a guarantee about the world.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** Held with confidence, or certain to occur.

**Active domains:** [COG], [LIN]

**Domain entries:**

- [COG] Subjective certainty, which can be well or badly calibrated.
- [LIN] As a reply, "sure" signals agreement, sometimes reluctant.

**Count condition:** A speaker's confident belief, or an outcome guaranteed.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Feeling sure is not the same as being right. "Sure" as agreement can be polite rather than enthusiastic.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-S-0004 | someone

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** Some person, not named or not known; also a person of importance.

**Example:** Someone left an umbrella at the door.

**RRGM bridge:** Someone holds the place of a person present in the event but absent from the record.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** An unidentified person.

**Active domains:** [LIN], [REL]

**Domain entries:**

- [LIN] Indefinite pronoun; singular, often referred back to with "they".
- [REL] "Someone should do something" names a need without naming who is responsible.

**Count condition:** A person whose identity is not given.

**Specific transfer boundary:** "Someone" leaves responsibility unassigned; it does not mean no one is responsible. Saying "someone told me" makes a claim hard to check.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-S-0005 | service

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** Work done for others; a system that supplies a public need such as transport or health care; a religious ceremony; or (in sport) a serve.

**Example:** The bus service is better now; the service at the café was quick.

**RRGM bridge:** A service is something done or supplied for others, judged by whether it actually meets their need.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** The act or system of doing work or providing something for others.

**Active domains:** [ECO], [REL], [DIG]

**Domain entries:**

- [ECO] Service industries sell work and expertise rather than goods.
- [REL] Service to others, and the duty of those who serve; "public service" names work done for a community.
- [DIG] A software service runs on servers and is reached over a network; terms of service set the rules of use.

**Count condition:** Work or provision delivered to others, or a formal ceremony.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Calling something a service does not mean it serves its users well. "Terms of service" are set by the provider and often favour it.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-S-0006 | shit

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** Vulgar: faeces; also used as an exclamation, an insult, or for things judged bad or worthless.

**Example:** Oh, shit, I missed the bus.

**RRGM bridge:** Shit takes the body's waste and turns it into a word for anything rejected, and into a release of frustration.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** A vulgar word for faeces, extended to rejection, worthlessness and strong exclamation.

**Active domains:** [BIO], [LIN], [PEJ]

**Domain entries:**

- [BIO] Faeces, the body's solid waste.
- [LIN] Highly versatile in informal speech: exclamation, intensifier ("scared shitless"), and general noun ("my stuff").
- [PEJ] Used as an insult ("a piece of shit"): it aims disgust at a person to mark them as worthless. The mechanism is transferring a bodily disgust response onto the target.

**Count condition:** Informal or vulgar speech, referring to waste, frustration or something judged bad.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Register matters: the word is offensive in many settings and ordinary in others. As an exclamation it describes the speaker's reaction, not a judgement of anyone.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]; [PEJ] PROVISIONAL

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-S-0007 | south

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** The direction toward the South Pole, opposite north; also the southern part of a place.

**Example:** Birds fly south for the winter.

**RRGM bridge:** South is a direction fixed by the Earth's axis, and a region named by where it lies.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** The direction toward the Earth's South Pole, or the region lying that way.

**Active domains:** [PHY], [ECO]

**Domain entries:**

- [PHY] A cardinal direction defined by the planet's rotation axis.
- [ECO] "Global South" names lower-income countries collectively, a political-economic grouping more than a geographic one.

**Count condition:** A direction or region oriented toward the South Pole.

**Specific transfer boundary:** "Global South" is not strictly geographic; Australia is in the south but not in the grouping. "Going south" informally means getting worse.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-S-0008 | several

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** More than two but not many; also separate or respective.

**Example:** Several people asked the same question.

**RRGM bridge:** Several counts a small plurality without fixing the number.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** An indefinite number greater than two but not large.

**Count condition:** A number of items more than two and fewer than many.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Several is vague by design; when the count matters, give the number.

## C2-S-0009 | series

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** A number of similar or related things coming one after another; also a set of television programmes or books.

**Example:** A series of storms hit the coast; I'm watching a new series.

**RRGM bridge:** A series links items in order, so each is read in light of those before it.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** An ordered sequence of related items.

**Active domains:** [FOR], [PHY], [LIN]

**Domain entries:**

- [FOR] In mathematics, the sum of the terms of a sequence; it may converge or diverge.
- [PHY] In a series circuit, components are connected one after another along a single path.
- [LIN] A run of episodes or volumes sharing characters or a subject.

**Count condition:** Related items arranged or occurring in order.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Things happening in a series need not cause each other; order is not causation.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** In a short time from now; early; quickly.

**RRGM bridge:** Soon promises nearness in time without a fixed point.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** Within a short time after the present or a stated moment.

## C2-S-0012 | son

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** A male child in relation to his parents.

**Example:** Their son starts school in September.

**RRGM bridge:** A son is named by relation: the word only exists as one end of a parent–child bond.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** A male offspring, named in relation to a parent.

**Active domains:** [REL], [BIO], [MYT]

**Domain entries:**

- [REL] Kinship role with expectations that vary by culture; adoptive and step-sons are sons by relation.
- [BIO] Male offspring.
- [MYT] "Son of" in many traditions names lineage or nature, as in "son of the morning".

**Count condition:** A male child named in relation to a parent.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Sonship is a relation, not only biology; adoption creates it. The role's expectations come from culture, not from the word.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-S-0013 | street

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** A public road in a city or town, usually with buildings along it.

**Example:** They live on a quiet street near the park.

**RRGM bridge:** A street is shared space between private doors, where strangers pass and neighbours meet.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** A public road lined with buildings in a settled area.

**Active domains:** [ECO], [REL]

**Domain entries:**

- [ECO] Streets carry traffic, trade and services; "high street" names a town's main shopping street.
- [REL] Public space shared by residents and passers-by; "street smart" means practical knowledge learned outside formal schooling.

**Count condition:** A public way in a town or city, with buildings along it.

**Specific transfer boundary:** A street is public, but what happens on it is still governed by law and by the people who live there.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-S-0014 | song

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** A short piece of music with words, meant to be sung; also the musical sounds of birds.

**Example:** She wrote a song for her brother's wedding.

**RRGM bridge:** A song joins words to melody so that meaning is carried by both, and is remembered by both.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** A piece of music for the voice, usually with words.

**Active domains:** [LIN], [BIO], [REL], [AFF]

**Domain entries:**

- [LIN] Lyrics carry meaning through rhythm and rhyme as well as words.
- [BIO] Birdsong serves to mark territory and attract mates.
- [REL] Songs carry shared memory, identity and ritual within communities.
- [AFF] Songs can evoke strong feeling in listeners. Provisional domain.

**Count condition:** Vocal music, with words or with patterned calls as in birdsong.

**Specific transfer boundary:** A song's words and its music can say different things; meaning comes from both together. Copyright protects a song's lyrics and melody as separate works.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]; [AFF] PROVISIONAL

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-S-0015 | study

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** To give time and attention to learning about a subject; also a piece of research, or a room for reading and work.

**Example:** She studies physics; a new study found the drug worked.

**RRGM bridge:** Study is attention kept on a subject long enough for understanding to build.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** Sustained effort to learn or investigate, or the report of such an investigation.

**Active domains:** [COG], [FOR]

**Domain entries:**

- [COG] Learning through reading, practice and review.
- [FOR] A research study has a design, a sample and results, and its conclusions are only as strong as its method.

**Count condition:** A learner and a subject, or an investigation with a method and findings.

**Specific transfer boundary:** "A study found" is not the same as "it is known": one study can fail to replicate. Studying something is not the same as having mastered it.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1
