## C2-I-0001 | international

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** Involving or existing between two or more countries.

**Example:** The conference drew researchers from forty countries; it was truly international.

**RRGM bridge:** International names a relation that crosses the boundary of a single nation's jurisdiction.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** Spanning or concerning more than one nation.

**Active domains:** [ECO], [REL]

**Domain entries:**

- [ECO] International trade, finance and supply chains connect national economies.
- [REL] International law, treaties and organisations govern relations between states.

**Count condition:** Two or more nations are involved or affected.

**Specific transfer boundary:** International is not global: two countries are enough. An international agreement binds only those who sign it.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-I-0002 | industry

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** The making of goods in factories; also a branch of economic activity; also hard work.

**Example:** The town grew around the steel industry.

**RRGM bridge:** Industry organises labour and machines so that goods are made at scale.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** Organised economic production, or a sector of it.

**Active domains:** [ECO], [REL]

**Domain entries:**

- [ECO] Sectors such as manufacturing, mining or software; industrialisation transformed economies.
- [REL] Industry groups lobby governments for rules that favour their members.

**Count condition:** A sector of production, or productive effort.

**Specific transfer boundary:** "The industry says" reports an interested party's view, not a neutral one.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-I-0003 | itself

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** Reflexive and emphatic form of it.

**Example:** The machine turns itself off.

**RRGM bridge:** Itself turns an action back onto a thing.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** Reflexive or emphatic pronoun for "it".

**Active domains:** [LIN], [DIG]

**Domain entries:**

- [LIN] Reflexive ("cleans itself") and intensifier ("the idea itself").
- [DIG] Self-reference in systems: programs that modify or describe themselves.

**Count condition:** A sentence where a thing acts on or is emphasised as itself.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Grammatical self-reference does not settle whether there is a self; it applies to thermostats and to people alike.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-I-0004 | internet

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** The global network connecting computers and devices, over which websites, email and other services run.

**Example:** She found the recipe on the internet.

**RRGM bridge:** The internet is a network of networks, a shared structure no single owner controls.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** The worldwide interconnection of computer networks using common protocols.

**Active domains:** [DIG], [ECO], [REL]

**Domain entries:**

- [DIG] Packet-switched networking over shared protocols (TCP/IP); the web is one service on it.
- [ECO] Internet access is now basic infrastructure, unevenly distributed.
- [REL] Online communities and the spread of information, true and false.

**Count condition:** The global network of networks, or services on it.

**Specific transfer boundary:** The internet is not the web; the web runs on it. Finding something online does not make it true.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-I-0005 | island

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** A piece of land surrounded by water; also something isolated.

**Example:** They sailed to a small island.

**RRGM bridge:** An island is land bounded on every side by water, and by extension anything set apart.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** Land surrounded by water, or an isolated area.

**Active domains:** [PHY], [BIO], [REL]

**Domain entries:**

- [PHY] Land masses formed by volcanoes, coral or rising seas.
- [BIO] Island species evolve in isolation, often into unique forms.
- [REL] "No man is an island": no one is wholly separate.

**Count condition:** Land fully surrounded by water, or something isolated.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Isolation breeds difference: island populations can diverge from mainland ones.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-I-0006 | immediately

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** At once; without delay; also directly, with nothing between.

**Example:** Call me immediately; the house immediately next door.

**RRGM bridge:** Immediately removes the gap between now and the act, or between two things.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** Without delay or intervening element.

**Active domains:** [LIN]

**Domain entries:**

- [LIN] Time and position adverb.

**Count condition:** An act done at once, or a thing directly adjacent.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Urgency signalled by "immediately" can be pressure as well as need.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-I-0007 | income

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** Money received, especially regularly, from work, business or investments.

**Example:** Her income rose after the promotion.

**RRGM bridge:** Income is what flows in, the inflow side of what a household or firm lives on.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** Money received over a period.

**Active domains:** [ECO]

**Domain entries:**

- [ECO] Wages, profits, interest and transfers; income tax; income inequality.

**Count condition:** Money received over time.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Income is a flow, not wealth: a high income can coexist with debt, and wealth with low income.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-I-0008 | insurance

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** An arrangement where a company pays for loss or damage in return for regular payments.

**Example:** The insurance covered the flood damage.

**RRGM bridge:** Insurance pools risk, so many small payments cover a few large losses.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** A contract transferring risk of loss to an insurer for a premium.

**Active domains:** [ECO], [FOR]

**Domain entries:**

- [ECO] Premiums, claims and risk pools.
- [FOR] Based on probability over many cases.

**Count condition:** A contract covering specified losses in exchange for payment.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Being insured is not being covered for everything; exclusions matter.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-I-0009 | interview

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** A formal meeting in which someone is asked questions, for a job, a news report or research.

**Example:** She has a job interview tomorrow.

**RRGM bridge:** An interview is a structured exchange of questions and answers, where the asker shapes what comes out.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** A meeting for questioning one party by another.

**Active domains:** [REL], [FOR]

**Domain entries:**

- [REL] The interviewer holds power over the framing.
- [FOR] Leading questions shape answers; research interviews avoid them.

**Count condition:** A question-and-answer meeting with a purpose.

**Specific transfer boundary:** An interview answer depends on how the question was asked.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-I-0010 | investment

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** Putting money, time or effort into something in the hope of a later return.

**Example:** The house was a good investment.

**RRGM bridge:** Investment gives up something now for an expected gain later.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** Commitment of resources for future return.

**Active domains:** [ECO], [REL]

**Domain entries:**

- [ECO] Stocks, property and capital investment.
- [REL] Emotional investment in a relationship or cause.

**Count condition:** Resources committed for an expected return.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Past returns do not guarantee future ones.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1
