## C2-E-0001 | east

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** The direction toward the rising sun, opposite west; also the eastern part of a place, or (as "the East") Asia.

**Example:** The sun rises in the east.

**RRGM bridge:** East is the direction of sunrise, and also a name others have given to a large and varied part of the world.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** The direction opposite west, or a region lying that way.

**Active domains:** [PHY], [REL]

**Domain entries:**

- [PHY] Cardinal direction, where the sun rises.
- [REL] "The East" was named from a European point of view; it lumps together very different cultures.

**Count condition:** A direction or region oriented eastward.

**Specific transfer boundary:** "The East" is not one culture; the term reflects where the namer stood.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-E-0002 | exactly

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** Precisely; without error; also used to agree strongly.

**Example:** The train left at exactly nine; "Exactly!"

**RRGM bridge:** Exactly closes the gap between a description and what it describes.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** With no deviation from the stated value or description.

**Active domains:** [FOR], [LIN]

**Domain entries:**

- [FOR] Precision without approximation.
- [LIN] As a reply, signals full agreement.

**Count condition:** A description, measure or claim meant to match precisely.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Saying "exactly" does not make a figure exact; precision has to be shown. Agreement with "exactly" may be enthusiasm rather than checked agreement.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1
