## C2-G-0001 | guy

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** Informal: a man; in the plural ("you guys"), often any group of people regardless of gender.

**Example:** Some guy at the counter helped me find the right cable.

**RRGM bridge:** Guy is a low-formality name for a person, chosen to signal ease rather than status.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** An informal term for a person, typically a man.

**Active domains:** [LIN], [REL]

**Domain entries:**

- [LIN] Casual register; "you guys" has widened to a general plural address in many dialects, though some hear it as gendered.
- [REL] Signals familiarity and informality between speakers.

**Count condition:** Informal speech referring to a person or, in the plural, a group.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Whether "guys" includes everyone depends on the speaker and listener; some groups find it inclusive and others don't. In British usage a "guy" is also an effigy burned on Bonfire Night.
