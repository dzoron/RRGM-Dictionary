## C2-U-0001 | university

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** An institution of higher education and research that grants degrees.

**Example:** He studied history at university.

**RRGM bridge:** A university gathers teaching and research under one structure, so that knowledge is both passed on and extended.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** A degree-granting institution of advanced teaching and research.

**Active domains:** [COG], [REL], [ECO]

**Domain entries:**

- [COG] Advanced study and the training of researchers.
- [REL] A community of scholars and students with its own governance and traditions.
- [ECO] Funded by fees, public money and research grants; costs to students vary widely by country.

**Count condition:** An institution recognised as granting degrees and carrying out research.

**Specific transfer boundary:** A university degree certifies completed study, not all knowledge; much learning happens outside universities. Prestige and quality are measured separately.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-U-0002 | usually

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** In most cases; normally.

**Example:** I usually take the bus.

**RRGM bridge:** Usually reports a pattern that holds most of the time, leaving room for exceptions.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** In the majority of cases or occasions.

**Active domains:** [FOR]

**Domain entries:**

- [FOR] A frequency claim: more often than not.

**Count condition:** A recurring situation in which one outcome happens most often.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Usually is not always; the exceptions are real.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1
