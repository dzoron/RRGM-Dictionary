## C2-O-0001 | official

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** Relating to an authority or public body; approved by authority; also a person holding a public position.

**Example:** The official results come out tomorrow; a government official spoke to reporters.

**RRGM bridge:** Official marks what an authority has endorsed, so it carries the authority's weight.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** Authorised by, or holding a position in, an authority.

**Active domains:** [REL], [FOR]

**Domain entries:**

- [REL] Officials act on behalf of institutions and are accountable to them.
- [FOR] An official figure is the authority's figure; its accuracy still depends on method.

**Count condition:** Something authorised by an authority, or a person in a public role.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Official is not the same as true: official figures and statements can be wrong or incomplete.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-O-0002 | oil

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** A thick liquid that does not mix with water; used as fuel, for lubrication or in cooking.

**Example:** Fry the onions in a little oil; the price of oil rose.

**RRGM bridge:** Oil is stored energy and slipperiness, the liquid that both powers and smooths.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** A viscous liquid insoluble in water, from plants, animals or fossils.

**Active domains:** [PHY], [ECO], [BIO]

**Domain entries:**

- [PHY] Hydrophobic hydrocarbons; reduces friction.
- [ECO] Crude oil is a major commodity shaping economies and politics.
- [BIO] Oil spills harm wildlife; burning oil releases carbon dioxide.

**Count condition:** A water-insoluble viscous liquid, or petroleum.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Cooking oil and crude oil share a name, not a use.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-O-0003 | ok

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** Informal: all right; acceptable; also used to show agreement.

**Example:** Is it OK if I leave early? — OK.

**RRGM bridge:** OK is the smallest yes: it accepts without praising.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** An expression of acceptance, adequacy or agreement.

**Active domains:** [LIN], [REL]

**Domain entries:**

- [LIN] Among the most widely used words in the world; also spelled "okay".
- [REL] Agreement in OK can be wholehearted or reluctant.

**Count condition:** A reply of acceptance, or a judgement of adequacy.

**Specific transfer boundary:** "OK" can mean agreement, or only "I heard you". "I'm OK" may not be the whole story.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-O-0004 | officer

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** A person holding a position of authority, especially in the armed forces or police; also an office-holder in an organisation.

**Example:** A police officer took our statement.

**RRGM bridge:** An officer holds delegated authority within a structure, with a rank that sets its limits.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** A holder of an office or commissioned rank.

**Active domains:** [REL]

**Domain entries:**

- [REL] Officers carry authority on behalf of an institution and answer to it.

**Count condition:** A person holding an office or rank.

**Specific transfer boundary:** The officer's authority belongs to the role and is limited by its rules.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1
