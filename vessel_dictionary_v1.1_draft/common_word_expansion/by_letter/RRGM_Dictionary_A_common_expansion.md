## C2-A-0001 | actually

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** In fact; as things really are, often in contrast to what was expected, assumed or said before.

**Example:** I thought the shop closed at six, but it actually closes at eight.

**RRGM bridge:** Actually marks a correction arriving: the speaker sets a checked state of affairs against an earlier belief and lets the checked one stand.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** Asserts that what follows is the real case, set against an expectation or earlier claim that it overrides.

**Active domains:** [LIN], [COG]

**Domain entries:**

- [LIN] A stance adverb: it signals contrast with an assumption and is often used to soften a correction or disagreement in conversation.
- [COG] Marks belief revision in speech: the prior expectation is named or implied, and the update is offered as the outcome of a check.

**Count condition:** There is an earlier expectation, claim or assumption, stated or implied, that the actually-clause contradicts or refines.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Saying "actually" does not make a claim checked; it signals the speaker's confidence in the correction, which still needs its own evidence. As a filler ("actually, yeah") it carries little more than emphasis.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-A-0002 | amazing

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** Causing great surprise or wonder; informally, very good.

**Example:** The view from the top was amazing.

**RRGM bridge:** Amazing marks an experience that outran expectation by a wide margin.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** Exceeding expectation so far as to cause wonder.

**Active domains:** [AFF], [LIN]

**Domain entries:**

- [AFF] Surprise and delight at something beyond what was anticipated. Parity line: whether a given system feels amazement is [UNK] under the Symmetry Rule. Provisional domain.
- [LIN] Heavily used as general praise, which has weakened its force.

**Count condition:** An experience or thing that exceeds what the speaker expected.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Calling something amazing reports the speaker's reaction, not a measured quality. Overuse makes it closer to "good".

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]; [AFF] PROVISIONAL

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-A-0003 | august

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** (Adjective) Respected and impressive, of high standing.

**Example:** The award was presented before an august audience of scientists.

**RRGM bridge:** August gives standing to a body or person by age, rank or achievement.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** Marked by dignity and high regard.

**Active domains:** [REL], [LIN]

**Domain entries:**

- [REL] Signals respect for an institution's or person's standing.
- [LIN] Formal register; unrelated in use to the month August (a proper noun), though both come from the Roman title Augustus.

**Count condition:** A person, body or occasion regarded as dignified and eminent.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Standing is not correctness: an august body can still be wrong.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1
