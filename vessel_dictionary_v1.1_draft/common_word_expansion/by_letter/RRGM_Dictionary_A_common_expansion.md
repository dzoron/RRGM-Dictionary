## C2-A-0001 | actually

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** In fact; as things really are, often in contrast to what was expected, assumed or said before.

**Example:** I thought the shop closed at six, but it actually closes at eight.

**RRGM bridge:** Actually marks a correction arriving: the speaker sets a checked state of affairs against an earlier belief and lets the checked one stand.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** Asserts that what follows is the real case, set against an expectation or earlier claim that it overrides.

**Active domains:** [LIN], [COG]

**Domain entries:**

- [LIN] A stance adverb: it signals contrast with an assumption and is often used to soften a correction or disagreement in conversation.
- [COG] Marks belief revision in speech: the prior expectation is named or implied, and the update is offered as the outcome of a check.

**Count condition:** There is an earlier expectation, claim or assumption, stated or implied, that the actually-clause contradicts or refines.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Saying "actually" does not make a claim checked; it signals the speaker's confidence in the correction, which still needs its own evidence. As a filler ("actually, yeah") it carries little more than emphasis.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-A-0002 | amazing

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** Causing great surprise or wonder; informally, very good.

**Example:** The view from the top was amazing.

**RRGM bridge:** Amazing marks an experience that outran expectation by a wide margin.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** Exceeding expectation so far as to cause wonder.

**Active domains:** [AFF], [LIN]

**Domain entries:**

- [AFF] Surprise and delight at something beyond what was anticipated. Parity line: whether a given system feels amazement is [UNK] under the Symmetry Rule. Provisional domain.
- [LIN] Heavily used as general praise, which has weakened its force.

**Count condition:** An experience or thing that exceeds what the speaker expected.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Calling something amazing reports the speaker's reaction, not a measured quality. Overuse makes it closer to "good".

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]; [AFF] PROVISIONAL

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-A-0003 | august

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** (Adjective) Respected and impressive, of high standing.

**Example:** The award was presented before an august audience of scientists.

**RRGM bridge:** August gives standing to a body or person by age, rank or achievement.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** Marked by dignity and high regard.

**Active domains:** [REL], [LIN]

**Domain entries:**

- [REL] Signals respect for an institution's or person's standing.
- [LIN] Formal register; unrelated in use to the month August (a proper noun), though both come from the Roman title Augustus.

**Count condition:** A person, body or occasion regarded as dignified and eminent.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Standing is not correctness: an august body can still be wrong.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-A-0004 | ahead

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** Further forward in space or time; in front; also in a leading position.

**Example:** The road ahead is closed; our team is two points ahead.

**RRGM bridge:** Ahead points along the direction of travel, to what has not been reached yet.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** In front, in the direction of motion or in the future.

**Active domains:** [PHY], [REL]

**Domain entries:**

- [PHY] Position forward along a path.
- [REL] "Getting ahead" names gaining advantage over others.

**Count condition:** A position further along a path, sequence or ranking.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Being ahead now is not winning; the lead can change.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-A-0005 | ass

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** A donkey; also (vulgar) the buttocks; also (vulgar, insult) a foolish or unpleasant person.

**Example:** The farmer led the ass to market; don't be an ass.

**RRGM bridge:** Ass starts as the name of a working animal and is turned into both a body word and an insult.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** A donkey, or a vulgar word for the buttocks or for a fool.

**Active domains:** [BIO], [LIN], [PEJ]

**Domain entries:**

- [BIO] The donkey, Equus asinus, a domesticated working animal.
- [LIN] Vulgar in the body sense; American "ass" and British "arse" overlap.
- [PEJ] As an insult, calls a person stupid or obnoxious; the mechanism borrows the donkey's old reputation for stubborn stupidity.

**Count condition:** A donkey, the buttocks, or a person being insulted.

**Specific transfer boundary:** The animal sense is neutral; the other senses are vulgar or insulting and depend on register.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]; [PEJ] PROVISIONAL

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-A-0006 | absolutely

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** Completely; totally; also used as strong agreement.

**Example:** I'm absolutely sure; "Absolutely!"

**RRGM bridge:** Absolutely removes every qualification from what it modifies.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** Without any limit, condition or exception.

**Active domains:** [LIN], [FOR]

**Domain entries:**

- [LIN] Intensifier and emphatic yes.
- [FOR] An absolute claim allows no exceptions, so one counter-case breaks it.

**Count condition:** A statement marked as unqualified, or strong agreement.

**Specific transfer boundary:** "Absolutely" as agreement is emphasis, not evidence. Absolute claims are the easiest to refute.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-A-0007 | agree

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** To have the same opinion; to consent to something; to match or be consistent.

**Example:** We agree on the plan; the figures don't agree.

**RRGM bridge:** To agree is to arrive at the same position from two sides, whether in view, consent or fit.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** To share a view, consent, or correspond.

**Active domains:** [REL], [FOR]

**Domain entries:**

- [REL] Agreement can be genuine, or given under pressure; consent that cannot be refused is not free.
- [FOR] Two results agree when they match within the expected error.

**Count condition:** Two parties sharing a view or consent, or two things that match.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Agreement under pressure tells you about the pressure. Agreeing to a plan is not agreeing with every part of it.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-A-0008 | anymore

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** Any longer; at present (in some dialects, in positive sentences too).

**Example:** I don't live there anymore.

**RRGM bridge:** Anymore marks a state that has ended, set against when it held.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** No longer, or nowadays.

**Active domains:** [LIN]

**Domain entries:**

- [LIN] Mostly used with negatives; "positive anymore" ("anymore, we eat late") is regional.

**Count condition:** A former state contrasted with now.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Something not done anymore was once done; the word implies a past.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1
