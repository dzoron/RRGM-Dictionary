## C2-V-0001 | various

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** Several different; of different kinds.

**Example:** The shop sells various kinds of tea.

**RRGM bridge:** Various gathers many different things under one word without listing them.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** Of several different kinds or instances.

**Active domains:** [LIN]

**Domain entries:**

- [LIN] Quantifier implying variety as well as number.

**Count condition:** Several items differing from one another.

**Specific transfer boundary:** "Various sources say" leaves the sources unchecked; name them where it matters.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-V-0002 | via

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** Travelling through a place on the way to another; also by means of.

**Example:** We flew to Rome via Paris; send it via email.

**RRGM bridge:** Via names the route or channel something passes through.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** By way of, or by means of.

**Active domains:** [DIG]

**Domain entries:**

- [DIG] The channel a message takes.

**Count condition:** A route or medium through which something passes.

**Specific transfer boundary:** The route is not the destination; a message sent via a channel can be read or changed there.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1
