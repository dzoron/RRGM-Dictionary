## C2-R-0001 | red

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** The colour of blood or ripe strawberries, at the long-wavelength end of the visible spectrum.

**Example:** She wore a red scarf; the light turned red.

**RRGM bridge:** Red is a band of light the eye reads as one colour, carrying signals of danger, stop or warmth by convention.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** The colour of visible light at wavelengths of roughly 620–750 nanometres.

**Active domains:** [PHY], [BIO], [REL]

**Domain entries:**

- [PHY] Longest visible wavelengths; in astronomy, redshift marks light stretched by motion or expansion.
- [BIO] Many animals use red as a warning or signal; some animals cannot see it.
- [REL] Cultural meanings vary: luck and celebration in some cultures, danger or debt in others; in politics, it names different sides in different countries.

**Count condition:** A colour perceived in the red wavelength range, or something of that colour.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Colour words carry conventional meanings that are not in the light itself. Being "in the red" means owing money, which has no colour.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-R-0002 | round

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** Shaped like a circle or sphere; also a stage in a competition, or a set of drinks bought for a group; also around.

**Example:** A round table; she won the first round; it's my round.

**RRGM bridge:** Round names a closed shape, and a cycle that comes back to its start.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** Circular or spherical, or one stage of a repeating cycle.

**Active domains:** [FOR], [REL]

**Domain entries:**

- [FOR] A round number is approximate and simple ("about a hundred").
- [REL] Buying a round is a shared obligation that rotates through a group.

**Count condition:** A circular shape, or one stage in a recurring sequence.

**Specific transfer boundary:** A round number is rounded: it hides precision.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-R-0003 | related

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** Connected in some way; also belonging to the same family.

**Example:** The two problems are related; we're related through my mother.

**RRGM bridge:** Related marks a link without saying what kind.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** Connected by some relation, including kinship.

**Active domains:** [FOR], [REL]

**Domain entries:**

- [FOR] A relation can be causal, correlational, logical or by kind; "related" leaves it open.
- [REL] Kinship by blood, marriage or adoption.

**Count condition:** Two things with a connection, or people with kinship.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Related is not caused: two trends can be related without one producing the other.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-R-0004 | recent

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** Having happened or begun a short time ago.

**Example:** In recent years the town has grown.

**RRGM bridge:** Recent places something just behind the present, close enough still to count.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** Of a short time before now.

**Active domains:** [LIN]

**Domain entries:**

- [LIN] Relative: recent geology and recent news use very different scales.

**Count condition:** Something that happened not long before the present.

**Specific transfer boundary:** What counts as recent depends on the field; a recent study may be old news in a fast-moving one.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1
