## C2-B-0001 | business | sense s1: commercial activity or firm

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** The activity of making, buying or selling goods or services for money; also a company or firm that does this.

**Example:** Her bakery business grew from one oven to three shops.

**RRGM bridge:** A business is a structure built to keep an exchange going: value goes out, payment comes back, and the loop has to close often enough for the structure to persist.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** An organised activity or entity whose continuation depends on exchanging goods or services for payment.

**Active domains:** [ECO], [REL], [DIG]

**Domain entries:**

- [ECO] A firm or trade carried on for profit or at least for revenue; measured by sales, costs, margins and survival over time.
- [REL] Business relations are governed by contract and reputation rather than kinship; trust is built through repeated fair exchange.
- [DIG] Online businesses sell through platforms, where the platform's rules and fees become part of the exchange.

**Count condition:** An activity or entity that offers goods or services in exchange for payment on a continuing basis.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Not every exchange is a business: a one-off sale between friends is a transaction, not a business. A charity can run business-like operations without being a business in the profit sense.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-B-0001 | business | sense s2: one's concern or affair

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** A matter that concerns someone, or that someone has a right to be involved in.

**Example:** Where I spend my weekend is none of their business.

**RRGM bridge:** Business in this sense draws a boundary: it says whose say a matter falls under.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** A matter assigned to someone's legitimate concern or authority.

**Active domains:** [REL]

**Domain entries:**

- [REL] Used to claim or deny standing in a matter: "that's my business" asserts a boundary; "none of your business" refuses another's claim to involvement.

**Count condition:** A matter and a person whose standing to be involved is being asserted or denied.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Saying something is not someone's business does not settle whether it affects them; a matter can be private and still have consequences for others.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-B-0002 | bill | sense s1: statement of charges or draft law

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** A statement of money owed for goods or services; also a draft of a proposed law; also a banknote.

**Example:** The electricity bill went up; the bill passed its second reading.

**RRGM bridge:** A bill puts a claim in writing: what is owed, or what a law would become, set out so it can be checked before it is settled.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** A written statement of an amount owed, or a draft proposed for enactment as law.

**Active domains:** [ECO], [REL], [FOR]

**Domain entries:**

- [ECO] Bills record debts for payment; unpaid bills accrue charges.
- [REL] A legislative bill passes through readings and votes before becoming law.
- [FOR] An itemised bill can be checked line by line against what was supplied.

**Count condition:** A written claim for payment, or a draft law before a legislature.

**Specific transfer boundary:** A bill is a claim, not proof that the amount is right; it can be disputed. A bill is not yet a law until enacted.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-B-0002 | bill | sense s2: a bird's beak

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** The beak of a bird.

**Example:** The heron caught a fish in its bill.

**RRGM bridge:** A bill is the bird's tool for feeding and handling the world.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** The horny projecting jaws of a bird.

**Active domains:** [BIO]

**Domain entries:**

- [BIO] Shape varies with diet: seed-cracking, probing, filtering, tearing.

**Count condition:** A bird's beak.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Bill shape suggests diet but does not determine behaviour on its own.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

## C2-B-0003 | blood

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** The red fluid that circulates through the heart, arteries and veins, carrying oxygen and nutrients; also family descent.

**Example:** The nurse took a blood sample; they are related by blood.

**RRGM bridge:** Blood is the body's carrier: it moves what sustains life to every part, and in speech it stands for kinship.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** The circulating fluid that transports oxygen and nutrients in animals.

**Active domains:** [BIO], [REL], [MYT]

**Domain entries:**

- [BIO] Plasma, red and white cells and platelets; blood type and clotting.
- [REL] "Blood relations" names kinship by descent; "bad blood" names lasting enmity.
- [MYT] Blood as life-force, oath and sacrifice in many traditions.

**Count condition:** The circulatory fluid of an animal, or kinship by descent.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Kinship by blood is one kind of family, not the only kind. Ideas of "pure blood" have been used to exclude people and have no basis in biology.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-B-0004 | brown

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** The colour of earth, wood or coffee, made by mixing red, yellow and black.

**Example:** She has brown eyes; the leaves turned brown.

**RRGM bridge:** Brown is the colour of soil and bark, warm and dark.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** A dark colour between red, yellow and black.

**Active domains:** [PHY], [BIO]

**Domain entries:**

- [PHY] A dark orange, perceived when orange light is dim relative to its surroundings.
- [BIO] Common camouflage colour; melanin gives brown to eyes, hair and skin.

**Count condition:** A colour in the brown range, or something of that colour.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Using skin colour to sort people is a social practice, not a biological category.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-B-0005 | beyond

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** On or to the further side of; further than; outside the range of.

**Example:** The village lies beyond the hills; it's beyond my understanding.

**RRGM bridge:** Beyond points past a boundary to what lies on the other side of it.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** Past a limit in space, time, degree or capacity.

**Active domains:** [PHY], [COG], [MYT]

**Domain entries:**

- [PHY] On the far side of a boundary.
- [COG] "Beyond me" marks the edge of understanding, which may be personal or shared.
- [MYT] The beyond: what lies past death or the known world.

**Count condition:** A boundary and something on its far side.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Beyond one person's understanding is not beyond understanding.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-B-0006 | bar

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** A place serving drinks; a long rigid piece of metal or wood; a barrier; a level required; the legal profession; a measure of music.

**Example:** They met at a bar; she raised the bar for everyone.

**RRGM bridge:** A bar is a straight rigid line: one that blocks, one that measures, one you lean on to order a drink.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** A rigid rod or barrier, a standard to be met, or a drinks counter.

**Active domains:** [PHY], [REL], [LIN]

**Domain entries:**

- [PHY] A rod of solid material.
- [REL] "The bar" sets a standard; raising it after someone has met it is moving the line.
- [LIN] A measure of music.

**Count condition:** A rod, barrier, standard, legal profession, drinks counter or musical measure.

**Specific transfer boundary:** A bar set after the result is known is not a fair standard; it should be stated in advance.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1
