## C2-W-0001 | woman

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** An adult female human being.

**Example:** The woman at the front desk gave us directions.

**RRGM bridge:** Woman names an adult female person, the counterpart to man.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** An adult female human.

**Active domains:** [BIO], [REL], [LIN]

**Domain entries:**

- [BIO] Adult human female.
- [REL] Social roles and expectations attached to women vary across cultures and have changed greatly over time; many have been challenged and revised.
- [LIN] Plural "women"; once also used in compounds where neutral terms are now common.

**Count condition:** An adult person who is female, or who identifies as a woman.

**Specific transfer boundary:** The roles expected of women come from culture, not from the word. Generalisations about "women" describe tendencies at most, not any individual.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-W-0002 | week

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** A period of seven days; also the working days of that period.

**Example:** I'll see you next week; she works a four-day week.

**RRGM bridge:** A week is a human-made cycle of seven days, a rhythm laid over time by convention.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** A unit of seven consecutive days.

**Active domains:** [ECO], [REL]

**Domain entries:**

- [ECO] The working week sets the pattern of labour and rest.
- [REL] Weekly rhythms (market days, rest days, worship) organise community life.

**Count condition:** A period of seven days, or the working portion of it.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Unlike the day or year, the week matches no astronomical cycle; it is a social convention.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-W-0003 | west

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** The direction toward the setting sun, opposite east; also the western part of a place, or (as "the West") Europe and North America.

**Example:** The sun sets in the west.

**RRGM bridge:** West is the direction of sunset, and also a name a group of countries gives itself.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** The direction opposite east, or a region lying that way.

**Active domains:** [PHY], [REL]

**Domain entries:**

- [PHY] Cardinal direction, where the sun sets.
- [REL] "The West" is a cultural and political grouping, not a fixed place; its boundaries are disputed.

**Count condition:** A direction or a region oriented westward, or the grouping called the West.

**Specific transfer boundary:** "The West" is defined by politics and history more than geography. Directions are relative: every place is west of somewhere.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-W-0004 | wife

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** A married woman considered in relation to her spouse.

**Example:** His wife is a doctor.

**RRGM bridge:** Wife names a role within a marriage, one end of a partnered bond.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** A woman in relation to the person she is married to.

**Active domains:** [REL]

**Domain entries:**

- [REL] Marriage roles and expectations vary across cultures and have changed over time; legal rights of wives have expanded greatly.

**Count condition:** A married woman, named in relation to her spouse.

**Specific transfer boundary:** The role does not define the whole person; the expectations attached to it come from culture and law.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-W-0005 | whatever

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** Anything or everything that; no matter what; also (informally) an expression of indifference.

**Example:** Take whatever you need; whatever happens, call me.

**RRGM bridge:** Whatever leaves the content open: any value will do.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** Any or every thing, with the choice left open.

**Active domains:** [LIN]

**Domain entries:**

- [LIN] Free relative and concessive; as a reply, can signal dismissal.

**Count condition:** An open choice, or indifference to the options.

**Specific transfer boundary:** As a reply, "whatever" can close a conversation without agreeing to anything.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1
