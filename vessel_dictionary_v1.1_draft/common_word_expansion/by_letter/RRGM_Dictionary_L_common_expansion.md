## C2-L-0001 | league

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** A group of sports teams that compete with each other; also an alliance of people or countries; also a level of quality.

**Example:** The team was promoted to a higher league; she's in a different league.

**RRGM bridge:** A league binds members into a structure of shared rules and ranking.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** An association of members bound by shared rules, often for competition.

**Active domains:** [REL], [ECO]

**Domain entries:**

- [REL] Alliances of people or states for common purpose.
- [ECO] Sports leagues are commercial structures with rules on teams and revenue.

**Count condition:** Members bound in an association, or a level of standing.

**Specific transfer boundary:** "Not in the same league" is a ranking claim, which depends on the measure used.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

## C2-L-0002 | lord

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** A man of noble rank or high authority; in some religions, a title for God or Christ; also a master or ruler.

**Example:** The lord of the manor owned the land; the Lord's Prayer.

**RRGM bridge:** Lord names a holder of power over land or people, and in faith a title of devotion.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** A person or being with authority or dominion over others.

**Active domains:** [REL], [MYT]

**Domain entries:**

- [REL] Feudal lords held land and owed duties to and from those under them.
- [MYT] Title of divinity in several traditions. Faith claims are held in their own domain.

**Count condition:** A nobleman, ruler or master, or a divine title.

**Specific transfer boundary:** A title of rank grants position, not merit. "Lording it over" someone means abusing a position over them.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1
