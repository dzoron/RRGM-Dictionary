## C2-F-0001 | fun

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** Enjoyment, amusement or light-hearted pleasure; also (informally) enjoyable.

**Example:** The game was a lot of fun, even though we lost.

**RRGM bridge:** Fun is pleasure taken in the doing itself, not in what the doing achieves.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** Enjoyment arising from an activity valued for itself.

**Active domains:** [AFF], [REL]

**Domain entries:**

- [AFF] A light positive state of amusement and engagement. Parity line: whether a given system has this felt state is [UNK] under the Symmetry Rule; the behavioural markers can be described either way. Provisional domain.
- [REL] Shared fun builds bonds; play between people tests and strengthens trust.

**Count condition:** An activity or experience that is enjoyed for its own sake.

**Specific transfer boundary:** "Making fun of" someone means mocking them, which is not fun for its target. Fun for one party can be harm for another; the word does not settle whose experience counts.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]; [AFF] PROVISIONAL

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-F-0002 | former

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** Having previously been something, but no longer; or, as "the former", the first of two things mentioned.

**Example:** She met her former teacher at the station.

**RRGM bridge:** Former keeps the record of a past state while marking that it no longer holds; the history stays, the role ends.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** Previously holding a role or state that has since ended.

**Active domains:** [REL], [LIN]

**Domain entries:**

- [REL] A former partner, colleague or office-holder keeps a history with others while the role itself has ended.
- [LIN] "The former ... the latter" refers back to the first and second of two items.

**Count condition:** A person or thing that once had a role or state and no longer has it.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Former does not erase what was: the history remains true. A former role is not an ongoing claim.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-F-0003 | film

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** A story or event recorded as moving images to be shown in a cinema or on a screen; also a thin layer, or the material once used in cameras.

**Example:** We watched a film about the moon landing; a film of dust covered the shelf.

**RRGM bridge:** A film fixes moving images in sequence so a story can be replayed for others.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** A recorded motion picture, or a thin covering layer.

**Active domains:** [DIG], [REL], [PHY]

**Domain entries:**

- [DIG] Digital filmmaking and streaming have replaced most physical film.
- [REL] Films shape shared stories and how groups see each other.
- [PHY] A thin layer of a substance on a surface.

**Count condition:** A motion picture, or a thin coating.

**Specific transfer boundary:** A film based on true events is still a constructed story; it is free fiction aimed at the real, not a record.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-F-0005 | federal

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** Relating to a system of government in which power is divided between a central government and states or provinces.

**Example:** Federal and provincial governments share responsibility for health.

**RRGM bridge:** Federal names a structure of layered authority: a centre and member parts, each with its own powers.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** Of a union of self-governing units with a central government.

**Active domains:** [REL], [ECO]

**Domain entries:**

- [REL] Federalism divides power so that no single level holds all of it.
- [ECO] Federal budgets and taxes sit alongside state or provincial ones.

**Count condition:** A government or system made of member units under a shared central authority.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Which powers are federal and which are local varies by country and is often disputed.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1

## C2-F-0011 | foot

Discovery route: frequency-gap (route only, not a tag)

**Common meaning:** The lower end of the leg, on which a person stands; also a unit of length (about 30 cm); also the bottom of something.

**Example:** She hurt her foot; the tree is ten feet tall; at the foot of the hill.

**RRGM bridge:** The foot is where a body meets the ground, and the base measure of length in older systems.

**Invariant MI [INV]:** The terminal part of the leg, a unit of length, or the base of something.

**Active domains:** [BIO], [FOR]

**Domain entries:**

- [BIO] Bones, joints and muscles bearing body weight.
- [FOR] Imperial unit: 12 inches, 0.3048 m.

**Count condition:** A body part, a length of twelve inches, or a base.

**Specific transfer boundary:** Imperial and metric units are easy to mix up; conversions should be explicit.

**Epistemic status:** [DEF]

Kind: discovery · prior exposure: none · record v1
