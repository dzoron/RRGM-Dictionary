@ C2-B-0002 | bill | s1 | statement of charges or draft law
cm: A statement of money owed for goods or services; also a draft of a proposed law; also a banknote.
ex: The electricity bill went up; the bill passed its second reading.
br: A bill puts a claim in writing: what is owed, or what a law would become, set out so it can be checked before it is settled.
inv: A written statement of an amount owed, or a draft proposed for enactment as law.
[ECO] Bills record debts for payment; unpaid bills accrue charges.
[REL] A legislative bill passes through readings and votes before becoming law.
[FOR] An itemised bill can be checked line by line against what was supplied.
cc: A written claim for payment, or a draft law before a legislature.
tb: A bill is a claim, not proof that the amount is right; it can be disputed. A bill is not yet a law until enacted.

@ C2-B-0003 | blood
cm: The red fluid that circulates through the heart, arteries and veins, carrying oxygen and nutrients; also family descent.
ex: The nurse took a blood sample; they are related by blood.
br: Blood is the body's carrier: it moves what sustains life to every part, and in speech it stands for kinship.
inv: The circulating fluid that transports oxygen and nutrients in animals.
[BIO] Plasma, red and white cells and platelets; blood type and clotting.
[REL] "Blood relations" names kinship by descent; "bad blood" names lasting enmity.
[MYT] Blood as life-force, oath and sacrifice in many traditions.
cc: The circulatory fluid of an animal, or kinship by descent.
tb: Kinship by blood is one kind of family, not the only kind. Ideas of "pure blood" have been used to exclude people and have no basis in biology.
