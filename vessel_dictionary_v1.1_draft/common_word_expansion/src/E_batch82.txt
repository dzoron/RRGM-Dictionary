@ C2-E-0003 | economic
cm: Relating to the economy, or to the production and use of money and resources; also profitable.
ex: The country faced economic problems.
br: Economic names the domain of how resources are made, moved and shared.
inv: Concerning the production, distribution and use of resources.
[ECO] Growth, employment, prices and trade.
[REL] Economic decisions distribute gains and losses among people.
cc: A matter of economy or resources.
tb: "Economic" (about the economy) differs from "economical" (thrifty). An economic gain overall can be a loss for many people.

@ C2-E-0004 | election
cm: The formal process of choosing a person or group for a position, especially by voting.
ex: The election is in October.
br: An election converts many individual choices into one recorded decision about who holds power.
inv: A formal choice of office-holders by vote.
[REL] Elections grant authority and allow peaceful transfer of power.
[FOR] Electoral systems differ: first-past-the-post, proportional, ranked choice; each turns the same votes into different results.
cc: A procedure in which eligible voters choose among candidates or options.
tb: Winning an election grants authority, not correctness. Who is allowed to vote decides whose choice counts.
