@ C2-I-0003 | itself
cm: Reflexive and emphatic form of it.
ex: The machine turns itself off.
br: Itself turns an action back onto a thing.
inv: Reflexive or emphatic pronoun for "it".
[LIN] Reflexive ("cleans itself") and intensifier ("the idea itself").
[DIG] Self-reference in systems: programs that modify or describe themselves.
cc: A sentence where a thing acts on or is emphasised as itself.
tb: Grammatical self-reference does not settle whether there is a self; it applies to thermostats and to people alike.

@ C2-I-0004 | internet
cm: The global network connecting computers and devices, over which websites, email and other services run.
ex: She found the recipe on the internet.
br: The internet is a network of networks, a shared structure no single owner controls.
inv: The worldwide interconnection of computer networks using common protocols.
[DIG] Packet-switched networking over shared protocols (TCP/IP); the web is one service on it.
[ECO] Internet access is now basic infrastructure, unevenly distributed.
[REL] Online communities and the spread of information, true and false.
cc: The global network of networks, or services on it.
tb: The internet is not the web; the web runs on it. Finding something online does not make it true.
