@ C2-D-0003 | department
cm: A division of a large organisation, such as a government, university, shop or company, dealing with a particular area.
ex: She works in the sales department.
br: A department is a section of a larger structure, given its own remit so the whole can divide its work.
inv: A specialised division of an organisation.
[ECO] Departments split budgets, staff and responsibility within an organisation.
[REL] Department boundaries shape who talks to whom, and can become barriers.
cc: A named division with its own area of responsibility within an organisation.
tb: A matter falling between departments can end up owned by none; "not our department" is a common way responsibility goes missing.

inv: A person responsible for directing an organisation, activity or production.

[ECO] Company directors have legal duties to the company.
