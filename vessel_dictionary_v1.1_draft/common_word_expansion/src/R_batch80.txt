@ C2-R-0001 | red
cm: The colour of blood or ripe strawberries, at the long-wavelength end of the visible spectrum.
ex: She wore a red scarf; the light turned red.
br: Red is a band of light the eye reads as one colour, carrying signals of danger, stop or warmth by convention.
inv: The colour of visible light at wavelengths of roughly 620–750 nanometres.
[PHY] Longest visible wavelengths; in astronomy, redshift marks light stretched by motion or expansion.
[BIO] Many animals use red as a warning or signal; some animals cannot see it.
[REL] Cultural meanings vary: luck and celebration in some cultures, danger or debt in others; in politics, it names different sides in different countries.
cc: A colour perceived in the red wavelength range, or something of that colour.
tb: Colour words carry conventional meanings that are not in the light itself. Being "in the red" means owing money, which has no colour.
