@ C2-T-0010 | technology
cm: The application of scientific knowledge to practical purposes; also machines and devices built that way.
ex: New technology made the factory safer.
br: Technology is knowledge turned into tools, which then reshape the people who use them.
inv: Practical methods and devices derived from knowledge.
[DIG] Computing and digital systems, often meant by "tech" today.
[ECO] Technology changes productivity, jobs and markets.
[REL] Who controls a technology shapes who benefits from it.
cc: Applied knowledge in methods or devices.
tb: Technology is neither good nor bad by itself; its effects depend on use and access. New is not automatically better.

k: discovery

[LIN] Deictic: depends on when it is said.
cc: The night of the day on which it is spoken.

@ C2-T-0012 | tomorrow

ex: I'll call you tomorrow.
br: Tomorrow is the next day, where plans and promises are placed.
inv: The day following the present day.
[LIN] Deictic: depends on when it is said.
