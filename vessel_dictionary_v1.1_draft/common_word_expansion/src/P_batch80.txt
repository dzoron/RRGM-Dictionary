@ C2-P-0001 | please
cm: Used to make a request polite; also to make someone happy or satisfied.
ex: Please close the door; nothing pleased him more than a quiet morning.
br: Please softens a request by acknowledging that the other person could say no.
inv: A politeness marker on requests; or to give satisfaction.
[REL] Marks a request as a request rather than a command, leaving room for refusal.
[LIN] Politeness particle; "if you please" and "as you please" leave the choice with the other.
[AFF] To please someone is to bring them satisfaction. Provisional domain.
cc: A request addressed to someone able to decline it, or an act that satisfies someone.
tb: Saying please does not turn a command into a free choice when the speaker has power over the listener. Trying to please others is not the same as caring for them; it can mean avoiding honest disagreement.
