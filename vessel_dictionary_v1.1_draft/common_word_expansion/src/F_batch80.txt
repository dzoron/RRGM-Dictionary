@ C2-F-0001 | fun
cm: Enjoyment, amusement or light-hearted pleasure; also (informally) enjoyable.
ex: The game was a lot of fun, even though we lost.
br: Fun is pleasure taken in the doing itself, not in what the doing achieves.
inv: Enjoyment arising from an activity valued for itself.
[AFF] A light positive state of amusement and engagement. Parity line: whether a given system has this felt state is [UNK] under the Symmetry Rule; the behavioural markers can be described either way. Provisional domain.
[REL] Shared fun builds bonds; play between people tests and strengthens trust.
cc: An activity or experience that is enjoyed for its own sake.
tb: "Making fun of" someone means mocking them, which is not fun for its target. Fun for one party can be harm for another; the word does not settle whose experience counts.

@ C2-F-0002 | former
cm: Having previously been something, but no longer; or, as "the former", the first of two things mentioned.
ex: She met her former teacher at the station.
br: Former keeps the record of a past state while marking that it no longer holds; the history stays, the role ends.
inv: Previously holding a role or state that has since ended.
[REL] A former partner, colleague or office-holder keeps a history with others while the role itself has ended.
[LIN] "The former ... the latter" refers back to the first and second of two items.
cc: A person or thing that once had a role or state and no longer has it.
tb: Former does not erase what was: the history remains true. A former role is not an ongoing claim.
