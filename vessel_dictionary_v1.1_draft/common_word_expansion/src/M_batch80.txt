@ C2-M-0001 | maybe
cm: Perhaps; possibly; used to show uncertainty or to make a suggestion softer.
ex: Maybe we should leave early to beat the traffic.
br: Maybe holds a possibility open without committing to it; it marks a claim as live but unweighed.
inv: Marks a proposition as possible but not asserted.
[LIN] Hedging adverb; also softens requests and suggestions.
[COG] Expresses uncertainty without a stated probability.
cc: A proposition the speaker holds as possible but does not assert.
tb: Maybe is not a probability; it could mean anything from unlikely to probable. Used as a polite refusal ("maybe another time"), it may mean no.

@ C2-M-0003 | market | s1 | place or system of trade
cm: A place where goods are bought and sold; also the whole system of buying and selling a kind of goods, or the demand for them.
ex: There's a farmers' market every Saturday; the market for used cars is strong.
br: A market is where offers meet demand and a price forms between them.
inv: A setting, physical or abstract, where buyers and sellers exchange and prices are set.
[ECO] Supply, demand and competition set prices; markets can fail, through monopoly, missing information or costs pushed onto others.

[DIG] Online marketplaces and app stores, where a platform controls access and takes a share.
cc: Buyers and sellers able to exchange, with prices that respond to them.
tb: A market price is what something sells for, not what it is worth in every sense. "The market decided" names an outcome of many choices; it does not mean no one decided.
